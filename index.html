How it’s built.

Vanilla JavaScript. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in one file. No framework and no build step.

Supabase. Goals, study logs and posts live in Supabase tables, and sign-in runs through Supabase functions.

WebGL. The aurora behind the page is a small fragment shader. If WebGL is unavailable, a CSS gradient takes over.

localStorage. Your theme, device mode and session are remembered in the browser.

Theme and device mode are saved in your browser.

Questions, answered.

Do I need an email address?

No. You pick a username and a password of at least six characters.

How does the study timer work?

Press Start when you sit down and Stop & log when you finish. The session is added to today’s total.

Will it run on an older phone?

Yes. Switch the device mode to Lower End and the blur, motion and WebGL background are turned off.

Where is my data stored?

Goals, study sessions and posts are stored in Supabase. Your theme and device mode stay in your browser.

Can teachers and students share one space?

Yes. The community feed is shared, and each post carries a Student or Teacher badge.

What is the command palette?

Press Ctrl+K (⌘K on Mac) to jump to a section, switch theme or device mode, or open the log-in window.
